Brazil Northeast Ecological Transformation Plan

Regional ecological transformation plan of the Northeast Consortium, created to territorialize the national ecological transition agenda in the nine states of the region, focusing on climate justice, bioeconomy, clean energy, sustainable financing and low-carbon productive development.

Partners: OEI, Open Society Foundations, Consórcio Nordeste

Details

Performance
Mapping and systematization of public policies in the nine states of the Northeast, territorial reading, survey of good practices and technical support for the organization of recommendations for a regional ecological transformation agenda.
Deliveries
Interviews, territorial diagnosis, technical reports, comparative analysis and subsidies for a strategy presented in the context of COP30, in dialogue with state workshops and interstate cooperation.
Themes
Sustainable financing, technological densification, bioeconomy, energy transition, circular economy, infrastructure, climate adaptation and valorization of knowledge and territorial assets in the Northeast.
