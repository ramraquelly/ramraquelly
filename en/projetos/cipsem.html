Details

Course
92nd UNEP/UNESCO/BMUV International Course on Sustainable Mobility: Transforming Urban Spaces, held by the Center for International Postgraduate Studies of Environmental Management (CIPSEM) at the Technische Universität Dresden.
Context
Program with professionals from 20 countries, combining classes, case studies, technical visits, country reports and negotiation exercises.
Themes
Sustainable mobility, urban planning, climate, public policies, transport and inclusive urban spaces.
