Project

Ministry of Culture / PRONAC

Technical evaluation of cultural projects

Acting as a technical evaluator and reviewer of cultural proposals, projects and action plans linked to PRONAC and cultural selection processes. The experience combines merit analysis, issuing opinions, participation in juries and technical reading of projects in culture, cinema, audiovisual, memory and territory.

Partners: Ministry of Culture, SEFIC/MinC, PRONAC

Details

What is it
Front for technical evaluation of cultural projects, working on proposals and action plans requested by the Ministry of Culture and related entities. The work involves reading the scope, adherence to criteria, methodological consistency, feasibility, cultural impact and issuing a technical opinion.
What did I do
Analysis of cultural proposals, participation in evaluation and jury processes, systematization of technical arguments and production of opinions. The work includes training at the MinC Reviewer Ambientation Workshop and accumulated experience in cultural assessment in recent years.
Evidence
SIAFI 2025 and 2026 commitment notes relating to analysis services and issuance of technical opinions, certificate from the MinC Reviewer Ambientation Workshop and certificates of participation in cultural and audiovisual processes.
Evaluation in other projects
Participation as a member of the technical jury for the 2026 ABC Cinematography Award, with analysis of 13 audiovisual works in the Photography Direction - Documentary Feature Film category. The documentation also records experiences related to the Rotas Negras UFSM Project and cultural selection processes, urban activation and new talents.
